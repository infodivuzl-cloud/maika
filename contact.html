Good to know

When can I order?

Orders open on 1 January 2027. You can already add products to your cart, and we’ll start delivering from January.

How do I place an order?

Add products to your cart, open the cart and go to checkout. Fill in your details and press “Place order on WhatsApp”. Your order arrives in our WhatsApp and we confirm the price and delivery with you.

How can I pay?

You can pay cash on delivery or by UPI on delivery, or we can send you a UPI link on WhatsApp.
