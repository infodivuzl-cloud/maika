Cookie Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short: maika.food uses no advertising, tracking or analytics cookies. We only save what the site needs to work, like your cart, and it stays on your own device.

What cookies and browser storage are

Cookies are small text files a website saves in your browser. “Local storage” is a similar space in your browser where a site can keep small notes, such as what is in your cart. Both stay on your device unless a site reads them.

What we use

  • Your cart (local storage, maika-cart): the products and quantities you add, so your cart is still there when you come back. Kept until you empty your cart or clear your browser’s site data.
  • Your delivery note (local storage, maika-note): saved only if you tick “Save this note for my next orders” at checkout.
  • This notice (local storage, maika-cookie-ok): remembers that you pressed “Okay”, so we don’t show it again.

These are strictly necessary for the site to work. They are not sent to us, and we don’t use them to follow you.

Cookies set by others

  • Razorpay: when you choose to pay online, Razorpay’s secure payment window opens and may set its own cookies to process the payment and prevent fraud. These are covered by Razorpay’s own privacy and cookie policies.
  • Google Fonts and Fontshare: provide the fonts on this site. They don’t set cookies, but your browser contacts their servers when a page loads.
  • Vercel: hosts this website and keeps short technical logs to run it securely. It doesn’t set tracking cookies on maika.food.

Links to WhatsApp and Instagram take you to those apps, where their own policies apply.

What we don’t use

No advertising cookies, no social media trackers, no analytics tools, and no selling of your information.

How to control them

You can clear cookies and site data at any time in your browser settings, for example in Chrome under Settings → Privacy and security → Delete browsing data. If you do, your cart will be emptied and this notice will appear again. Blocking storage completely may stop the cart from working.

Changes and questions

If we ever change what we use, we will update this page and the date at the top. Questions? Write to eat@maika.food or message us on WhatsApp at +91 92206 60377. For everything else about your information, see our Privacy Policy.
