About us

Maika is the dabba Maa packs when you leave home.

Every time we left home after the holidays, Maa packed a steel dabba of pinni and a jar of her mixed achaar, wrapped in newspaper "so the oil doesn't leak". It never lasted the week.

She still makes every batch in our kitchen, with desi ghee, gur and the same old kadhai. Small batches, made when the season is right, packed the way she packs for her own children.

Maika means a woman's mother's home: the place you are always welcome back to.

— Maa & family
