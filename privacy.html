Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

In short: we only use your details to make and deliver your order. Your cart stays on your own device, your order reaches us through WhatsApp, and we never sell your information.

Who we are

Maika is a small home kitchen in India making laddoos, pinni and spreads in small batches. This website is maika.food. If you have any question about your information, write to eat@maika.food or message us on WhatsApp at +91 92206 60377.

What information we collect

  • When you place an order: your name, phone number, email (if you give it), delivery address, preferred delivery date and time, payment choice and any note you add. The checkout page puts these into a WhatsApp message; we receive them only when you press send in WhatsApp.
  • When you sign up to be notified: your email address. The form opens your own email app, and we receive it only if you send that email.
  • When you contact us: whatever you choose to share in your message, call or email.

If you pay online, your payment is handled by Razorpay; we never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. Razorpay shares your name, phone, email, order details and payment status with us so we can deliver your order. We do not create customer accounts.

What stays on your device

Your cart, and your delivery note if you tick “Save this note for my next orders”, are saved in your browser’s local storage so they are still there when you come back. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can remove it at any time by emptying your cart or clearing your browser’s site data.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use analytics tools on this website. See our Cookie Policy for the full list.

How we use your information

  • To confirm your order, its price and your delivery.
  • To prepare, pack and deliver your order, and to contact you about it.
  • To let you know when orders open, if you asked us to.
  • To keep the records the law requires, for example for tax and food safety.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for anyone else’s marketing.

Who else handles your information

  • Razorpay: processes online payments under its own privacy policy and Indian payment regulations.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): carries the order messages you send us. Its own privacy policy applies to how it handles those messages.
  • Delivery partners: receive only your name, phone number and address, so they can deliver your order.
  • Vercel: hosts this website. Like any web host, it keeps short technical logs (such as your IP address and browser type) to run the site securely.
  • Google Fonts and Fontshare: provide the fonts on this website, so your browser contacts their servers when a page loads.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your order details for as long as we need them to complete your order and to look after any questions about it, and for longer only where the law requires us to keep records. When we no longer need them, we delete them.

Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it, where we are not required to keep it;
  • stop sending you messages about new orders.

To do any of these, or to raise a concern, write to eat@maika.food. We will reply as soon as we can. If you are not happy with our answer, you can contact the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

Our website is meant for adults placing orders. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to order for you.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
