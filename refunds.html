Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

In short: cancel before we start cooking for a full refund. Because this is fresh food, we cannot take returns, but if anything arrives damaged, wrong or not right, tell us within 24 hours with a photo and we will replace it or refund you.

Cancelling an order

  • Before we start preparing your order: full refund. To cancel, write to eat@maika.food or WhatsApp us on +91 92206 60377 with your order or payment ID.
  • After we have started preparing or dispatched it: we cannot cancel, because the food is made fresh for you.
  • If we have to cancel your order (for example, a batch sells out), you get a full refund automatically.

Returns

For food safety, we cannot accept returns of food once it has been delivered.

Damaged, wrong or missing items

  • Tell us within 24 hours of delivery, with a photo of the item and the packaging.
  • We will send a replacement or give a full refund for the affected item, including delivery if the whole order was affected.
  • If a product is not right in taste or quality, tell us within 24 hours. We look at every case and make it right.

How refunds are paid

  • Online payments: refunded to the same card, UPI account, bank or wallet you paid with. We start the refund within 2 working days of approving it; your bank usually takes 5–7 working days to show it.
  • Pay-on-delivery orders: refunded by UPI or bank transfer to an account you give us.

Questions

For any refund or cancellation question, write to eat@maika.food or WhatsApp us on +91 92206 60377. Please include your order or payment ID.
