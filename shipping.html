Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

In short: delivery is ₹79 per order, free from ₹999. We prepare your order fresh and dispatch it within 1–2 working days of your chosen delivery date. Orders open on 1 January 2027.

Where we deliver

We deliver across India, with a focus on Delhi NCR and Mumbai. If a courier does not serve your PIN code, we tell you before dispatch and refund you in full.

Delivery charges

  • ₹79 per order.
  • Free when the order total reaches ₹999.
  • The delivery charge is shown at checkout before you pay.

When your order arrives

  • We cook in small batches, so you choose a delivery date at checkout (from 1 January 2027).
  • We dispatch within 1–2 working days of that date. Delivery usually takes 1–3 days in Delhi NCR and 3–7 working days elsewhere in India.
  • Festival weeks (Lohri, Makar Sankranti) are busy, so please order early for gifts.
  • We share tracking details on WhatsApp once your order is dispatched.

Delivery problems

  • Please make sure someone can receive the order and the phone number is reachable.
  • If a parcel is delayed or lost by the courier, we follow it up and replace or refund it.
  • If an order cannot be delivered because the address was wrong or nobody was available, we contact you to arrange another delivery; a second delivery charge may apply.

Questions

For any delivery question, write to eat@maika.food or WhatsApp us on +91 92206 60377.
